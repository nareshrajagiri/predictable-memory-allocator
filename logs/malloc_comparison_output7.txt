Test: Behavioral comparison between malloc/free and pool allocator

=== malloc/free behavior ===
malloc allocated 1024 blocks
malloc/free cycle completed

=== pool allocator behavior ===
pool_alloc allocated 1024 blocks
pool_alloc returned NULL after exhaustion (expected)
pool_alloc/free cycle completed

Observations:
- malloc allocation success depends on system memory availability
- malloc does not expose a deterministic exhaustion boundary
- pool allocator enforces a fixed upper bound on allocations
- pool allocator fails deterministically after pool exhaustion
- pool allocator recovers correctly after free
